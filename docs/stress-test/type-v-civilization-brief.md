# Stress-test brief: TYPE V — BEYOND THE UNIVERSE

Provided by Victor for the end-to-end stress test of MotionCraft (budget 5 USD, Kie preferred, others as needed, no excess). The text below is
the brief exactly as given.

**Project:** TYPE V — BEYOND THE UNIVERSE
**Duration:** 10 seconds
**Format:** 16:9, 4K, 24 fps

## Creative brief

Create a sleek motion-graphics sequence imagining a **Type V civilization: a speculative civilization capable of operating across multiple universes**. Present this as science-fiction worldbuilding; Type V is an extrapolation beyond the original Kardashev scale.

The emotional journey is **curiosity → awe → quiet mastery**. Convey immense power through precision, scale, and restraint.

Begin with a single luminous point. Reveal it as one universe within a vast arrangement of translucent cosmic spheres. An elegant geometric structure connects them, suggesting a civilization shaping the relationships between universes.

Use obsidian black, platinum white, deep indigo, and a restrained electric-cyan accent. Combine crisp vector typography, dimensional geometry, subtle volumetric depth, and smooth camera movement. Keep the imagery clean, with controlled highlights and generous negative space.

Typography should remain native and editable. Use **Space Grotesk Medium** for the title and **Inter** for the supporting line.

## Timed script

| Time | Visual and movement | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:02** | A tiny white point emerges from black. The camera approaches; delicate concentric geometry forms around it. | **BEYOND A SINGLE UNIVERSE** | A quiet, crystalline pulse rises from silence. |
| **0:02–0:05** | The point expands into a translucent universe sphere. A continuous camera pullback reveals several distinct spheres suspended in deep space. | First line dissolves cleanly. | A deep tonal swell introduces scale. |
| **0:05–0:08** | Precise luminous paths connect the spheres. They align into an immense, elegant geometric constellation. Movement settles into controlled harmony. | **TYPE V** | A restrained bass impact at the reveal, followed by a widening harmonic chord. |
| **0:08–0:10** | Camera eases to a stop. The constellation remains behind the title, softly illuminated. Hold the final composition for readability. | **CIVILIZATION** beneath **TYPE V**. Small supporting line: **A speculative multiversal future** | Chord resolves into a clear, delicate resonance. |

## Optional narration

> "Beyond one universe—a civilization shaping many."

Deliver slowly with restrained wonder. Keep narration optional so the sequence also works with music and sound design alone.

## Build instructions

Use separate editable layers for titles, universe spheres, connecting paths, atmosphere, camera, music, and sound effects. Maintain one continuous camera move through the main reveal. Avoid rapid cuts, decorative HUD graphics, excessive particles, heavy bloom, and text embedded in generated imagery. End on a clean two-second title hold.
