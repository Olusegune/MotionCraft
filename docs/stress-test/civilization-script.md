# Civilization I–VII | What Would We Become?

**MotionCraft script · 90 seconds · 21:9 · Sparse narration**

Keep the original journey from a city window to the birth of a universe. At every scale, show someone doing something ordinary: coming home from work, adjusting a coat, or waiting for a friend. The technology and the way people inhabit it tell us which civilization we’re in.

**Visual thread:** A gold light passes from shot to shot. It starts as a kitchen lamp, becomes a worker’s helmet light, and ends as the first light in a universe. At the end, it brings us back to a window.

### How the humans could look

The early types can still look like us. From Type III onward, show a mix of familiar people, bodies adapted to particular habitats, and people who choose to appear through technology. A Type VI paramedic can look more recognizably human than a Type V café customer. That variety makes the world feel inhabited and keeps the civilization types tied to **what societies can harness**, rather than treating them as stages of human evolution.

For the **21:9 image pass**, give each frame a person and a readable action in the foreground, with the civilization’s scale revealed behind or beside them. The tailor tugging at a short sleeve, the café guest reaching for warmth, and the paramedic holding a child’s hand are the images to build around.
