# QuickPath: what is next

The plan, in the order I would do it. Items 1 and 2 need something from you.

## 1. Bring in your motion graphics script (needs your script)

QuickPath turns an idea into scenes. A motion graphics script is more exact: it says what appears when. The plan is to let you paste or open a timed script and
have each row become a scene, with the time you gave it, the words to show, and the picture or motion described.

I will shape the format around **your** script, so please send one when you have it. Likely rows look like this:

| Time | On screen | What we see | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:04 | WAKE UP SLOWLY | steam rising from a cup | soft piano |

The main MotionCraft app already reads this kind of table (and that code can be reused). What I need to see first: how your script is laid out (table, plain text,
Word or PDF), whether times are given, and how you mark voiceover and music. Never paste your keys into chat: enter them in Settings (see `SETUP_KEYS.md`).

## 2. Put your keys in Settings and try the paid parts for real

Kie voices, Suno music, pictures and animated clips were all tried live on 2026-11-26 (about $0.62 in total) and worked. Next is a full run on your script within the spending limit.

## 3. A person runs the manual test script

`QA.md`, section 7: 31 steps for a clean Windows PC and a Mac. Whatever breaks becomes the next fix list.

## 4. Release basics

- **Signing:** a Windows code-signing certificate and an Apple Developer account, so Windows and macOS stop saying "unknown publisher". (Costs money; your decision.)
- **Intel Mac** build (today only Apple Silicon is built).
- **Automatic updates** for the desktop app.

## 5. Sound

- Make OpenAI and Windows voices the same loudness.
- A different voice for one scene; speed and pitch.
- Listen to, and improve, the four built-in music moods (I can measure them but not judge them).

## 6. Pictures and clips

- Check an AI picture for stray words and try again automatically.
- AI video clips from a scene's picture (the main app already does this; it needs the same price confirmation).
- A "brand kit": your logo, colours and fonts, applied to every scene.
- More looks, and a choice of fonts.

## 7. Hand-off

Open the finished video in After Effects or DaVinci Resolve with the layers intact (the main MotionCraft app already writes these files; QuickPath projects are EffectCraft
projects, so the same route applies).

## What I will not do without asking

Spend money beyond your limit, change your keys in the main MotionCraft app, send anything to a provider that you have not confirmed, or publish anything.
